Proposed notes for the final build after login verification:

Community Car Spots is a community app for car enthusiasts, with places,
events, groups, chats, profiles and garages. An internet connection is required.

For review, choose Sign in with email on the sign-in screen and use the review
credentials entered above. Google, Telegram and Apple sign-in are also available
where supported. This is an ordinary account with no administrator privileges.

If onboarding appears, complete the profile fields and read and accept the
Terms of Use to continue. Existing accounts that have already accepted the
current terms should proceed without being asked again.

Use Spots or Map to browse places; Community contains groups and conversations;
Profile contains the garage and account settings. Available content varies by
region. The map can be moved to Riga, Latvia to inspect existing places without
travelling there. Location permission supports nearby places and location-based
features. Live-location sharing is optional and must be explicitly enabled.
Camera/photo access is used only when the corresponding feature is selected.

The app provides reporting and user blocking. Account deletion is available in
Settings. It requires confirmation and may require recent sign-in verification.
After an accepted request, automated cleanup continues while signed out; the
sign-in screen offers Check deletion status. Do not delete the supplied review
account before completing the rest of the review, as deletion is irreversible.
