CCS content-rights audit — 5 October 2026

Team artwork
The operator confirmed in this conversation that built-in photographs, badges,
logos and partner images were all created by the team. Record the confirmation
as provenance; it is not an independent audit of every image or depicted mark.

Third-party components still exist despite that confirmation:
- OpenFreeMap globe: commercial use permitted by the provider. Attribution is
  required. Current renderer uses the source attribution control, compact=false.
  Keep OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap attribution and working external links.
  https://openfreemap.org/
- MapLibre GL JS: licence text is present in assets/globe/LICENSE-maplibre.txt,
  within the bundled assets/globe directory. Do not remove the licence file.
- Natural Earth country outlines: repository provenance file identifies the
  source and public-domain status. Official terms confirm public-domain data.
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Social glyphs: assets/social/README.md identifies Simple Icons (CC0).
  CC0 does not grant trademark ownership. Use logos only to identify services.
- Material/Cupertino icons and Flutter dependencies: preserve the dependencies'
  packaged licence notices and review the final archive's notices.
- All map screens now use the OpenFreeMap globe renderer in the next app build.
  Provider attribution and bundled MapLibre licence remain included.

User uploads
The Terms contain a prospective licence for content submitted after acceptance,
an ownership/permission requirement, and a complaints contact. They explicitly
do not retrospectively obtain permission for historical uploads.
The owner's team-artwork confirmation does not cover all users' uploaded photos.
The local rules allow spot creation without a legal-acceptance document, as
reproduced in the local emulator. Fix enforcement and establish permission for
historical content before representing the entire audit as complete.

App Store Connect
Content Rights is still unset, as verified in the live console on 5 October.
Do not choose "no third-party content" merely because team artwork is original:
the app displays third-party map data and user contributions. Complete the
rights declaration only when the remaining historical-upload checks
are resolved. No Content Rights assertion was submitted during this audit.
