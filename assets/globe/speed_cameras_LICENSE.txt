Latvia fixed speed cameras

Source: OpenStreetMap contributors, extracted from the Geofabrik Latvia extract.

The bundled JSON is a derived database of highway=speed_camera nodes.
It contains mapped fixed cameras, not live police reports or mobile radar data.
Coverage can be incomplete or outdated. Average-speed sections are not imported.
No speed limits are imported or displayed.

Refresh with tools/import_speed_cameras.py and a current Latvia .osm.pbf extract
(requires Python osmium). Validate the output and commit it with the next app
release. Phones do not contact Overpass or Geofabrik and receive updates through
app releases. Existing OpenStreetMap attribution remains available on the map;
the camera card also links to the copyright and licensing page.
